# GoBYE website photo brief

The website photo section (`people` in `apps/web/site.config.js`) and the final banner currently use stills cut from the owner's hero video. They are real footage but low resolution (1024×576 source). Replace them with this shoot when it is ready. Drop the files into `apps/web/assets/people/` and change `photo`, `alt` and `pos` in the config. Nothing else needs to change.

## Rules

- Real people, photographed with their written consent (model release). Don't present them as GoBYE customers or drivers by name, and don't add quotes.
- Place the logo **physically** in the scene (car door magnet, windscreen sticker, lanyard, cap, phone screen). Never paint it onto a photo of a stranger or someone else's car.
- Show the app screen with the current build (Home "Where are you going?", the fare screen, or "Your driver is on the way").
- Warm golden hour, shallow depth of field, Lagos and Abuja streets. No police, no accidents, no cash changing hands.
- Deliver at least 2400 px on the long side, JPG, sRGB, with a portrait (4:5) and landscape (16:10) crop of each.

## AI image prompt (if you generate instead of shooting)

Use the prompt below with your image tool. A generated image is a concept: label it as an illustration, or reshoot it before launch.

> Photorealistic, golden hour on a busy Lagos road, Victoria Island skyline soft in the background. A young Nigerian woman in smart casual clothes stands at the roadside holding a smartphone; the screen shows a clean white ride-hailing app with a map and "Where are you going?". A silver Toyota Corolla is pulling up behind her with a small black-and-orange "GoBYE" magnet on the front door. Natural skin tones, shallow depth of field, 35 mm lens, candid, no other text or logos, no watermarks. 4:5.

The GoBYE logo must be added from the supplied logo file afterwards or placed physically. Image tools invent lettering, so never trust generated text.
